## ⭐ Featured: Third Street Bookmarks for macOS

**[third-street-bookmarks-macapp](https://github.com/mayanksagar26/third-street-bookmarks-macapp)** — a local-first **Pocket alternative** for the way people save things now. Your X/Twitter bookmarks, Hacker News, YouTube playlists, Instagram saves and loose links in one searchable, auto-categorised library on your Mac. Chat with your bookmarks (answers cite their sources), turn any folder into a podcast digest, and categorise offline with a small model trained on your own labels.

- 🔒 Nothing leaves your Mac — no account, no API key, no upload
- 🤖 Bring your own AI: runs on your local Claude Code or Codex CLI
- 🖥️ Native `.app` / `.dmg` for Apple Silicon (v1.2.0), built with Tauri 2 + React + Node

[**Download for macOS →**](https://github.com/mayanksagar26/third-street-bookmarks-macapp/releases/latest) · prefer a browser? [third-street-bookmarks](https://github.com/mayanksagar26/third-street-bookmarks)

## Projects

### Local-first apps

🧹 **[menlo](https://github.com/mayanksagar26/menlo)** — A calmer place for your files. A native macOS app that clears Downloads and Desktop into the folders you choose, by rules you write in plain words — *"anything with the word lease goes here"*. Shows every landing before anything moves, catches byte-identical duplicates, gives screenshots clearer names, and every run can be undone. Works with no AI at all; v0.1.0 for Apple Silicon. Tauri + Rust + React.

🔖 **[third-street-bookmarks](https://github.com/mayanksagar26/third-street-bookmarks)** — The browser build of the featured app above: same library, served from a small Node + React server on `localhost`. Both builds share `~/.tsb`, so they open the same collection.

🎬 **[vid-bid](https://github.com/mayanksagar26/vid-bid)** — Swap the product inside a video: upload a clip or paste a YouTube link, pick a detected object, give it a photo of your product, and get the same video back with your product in its place. Free and open source end to end, with no paid APIs, keys or accounts. A fast engine wraps the product onto the tracked shape (SAM 2 + CLIP + YOLO-World), and a realistic engine re-renders the region with the open Wan 2.1 VACE model on your own machine. Includes a CLI and a free Colab GPU notebook. Python + FastAPI.
